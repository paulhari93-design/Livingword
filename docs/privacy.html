Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

In short: LivingWord has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Your highlights and settings stay on your phone.

About the app

LivingWord is a free reader for Tamil translations of the messages of William Marrion Branham and for the Tamil and English (KJV) Bible. You can use it without signing in.

Information stored on your phone

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the messages and Bible text you download,
  • your highlights,
  • your reading settings, such as theme, font size and Bible language.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

Downloading messages and the Bible

When you agree to download, the app fetches the text from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The app checks for new or corrected messages when it opens. Like any internet service, Google's servers receive technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the data. We do not use this to identify you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to tell you when a new message is available. Firebase creates a random device identifier (a messaging token) so the notification can reach your phone. It is used only to deliver these notifications and is not linked to your name or any account. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Permissions

  • Internet — to download messages and the Bible.
  • Notifications — to tell you about new messages (optional).

Third-party services

Firebase is provided by Google. Its handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, write to the developer email address shown on the LivingWord page in Google Play.
